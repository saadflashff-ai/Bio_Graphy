Biography

Fatima Jinnah was the younger sister of Muhammad Ali Jinnah, the founder of Pakistan, and is known as Madar-e-Millat, “Mother of the Nation”. She was born on 31 July 1893 and died on 9 July 1967. Of Jinnah’s six siblings, she was the closest to him. Most sources place her birth in Karachi, though a few give other locations.

Unusually for a Muslim girl of her time, she was sent to school and then to professional training. She qualified as a dentist at Dr. Ahmad Dental College in Calcutta (graduating in 1923) and opened her own clinic in Bombay. After the death of her brother’s wife, Ruttie, in 1929, she moved into his household and helped raise his daughter, Dina. From then on she was his closest companion, and she accompanied him on political tours and during his years in London.

After Pakistan was created in 1947, she stayed close to her brother during the first difficult year, when the new state faced a refugee crisis and a lack of resources. She was with him until his death in September 1948.
