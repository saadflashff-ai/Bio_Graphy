Struggles

Neymar’s career has been shaped by injuries and criticism. At the 2014 World Cup he fractured a vertebra in the quarter-final against Colombia, ending his tournament. Foot and ankle injuries kept him out of key matches for PSG, and in 2023 he tore his knee ligaments while playing for Brazil. After that he made very few appearances for Al Hilal. Back at Santos in 2025, he battled injuries while the club fought relegation, then helped it stay in the top flight in the final matches. He had a minor knee operation in December 2025 and scored 11 goals in 34 appearances that season.

At the 2026 World Cup, muscle injuries limited him to two of Brazil’s five games. Some observers also criticise his temperament and his habit of going down easily, and he has served disciplinary suspensions. A Spanish court case over his 2013 transfer to Barcelona ended with his acquittal.

Key Contributions and Achievements
Brazil’s top scorer. He is Brazil’s all-time leading scorer with 80 goals, passing Pelé’s total of 77. 
ESPN
A member of the “MSN” attack with Lionel Messi and Luis Suárez at Barcelona, one of the most prolific forward lines in football history.
Trophy winner at the highest level. He won the Champions League, domestic league titles in Spain and France, and the Copa Libertadores.
Olympic hero. He captained Brazil to its first Olympic football gold in 2016, scoring the winning penalty in the final against Germany.
Four World Cups. The 2026 tournament was his fourth for Brazil.

Legacy

Neymar is widely seen as one of the most gifted forwards of his generation and a symbol of Brazil’s attacking tradition. Debate continues over how many major individual titles he might have won without injuries, which gives your page a balanced view.
